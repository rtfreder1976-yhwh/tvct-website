## What works

- 2026-08-23 — Christen "really liked" the teach-one-thing Facebook post (dishwasher filter → "that one's a freebie; the checklist is what we charge for"). Pattern: useful tip first, wry turn to the offer second. (foundation-voice-samples #5)

## What doesn't work

- 2026-08-23 — Describing price in time terms ("how long a 44-item clean takes"). Todd: price = square footage × type of cleaning; nothing hourly. Also: re-clean copy must say the client tells us ("tell us within 24 hours").

- 2026-08-23 — "Arithmetic" in a GBP post: pretentious, not a customer word. Plain-word test now in voice-profile.
- 2026-08-23 — Any line implying we measure/inspect the house ("if the square footage turns out different"). Christen: cleaner calls office → Christen calls client before anything changes.
- 2026-08-23 — Blunt rejection in wrong-fit copy ("it won't be us"); Christen wants scarcity/selectivity instead.

- 2026-08-24/25 — **Orchestrator inferences got published as fact, twice.** "Todd, an Army veteran" (he is **Air Force**) reached ~21 live files via a voice sweep, and "biweekly is the money segment" shaped the ICP while the real book is monthly-dominant. Both traced to provisional notes in brand files that were never marked provisional hard enough. Rule: anything tagged `[inferred]` must not enter site copy without Todd confirming it.
- 2026-08-24 — **Buyer-coaching copy that TVCT itself fails is worse than no copy.** Five best-company pages told readers to demand W-2 employees, bonding, and workers' comp — three things TVCT does not offer (subcontractors, not bonded, workers' comp unverified). Replaced with the who-takes-responsibility question, which TVCT answers well ($2M liability, certificate on request).

## Audience insights

- 2026-08-23 — Owner roles: Todd (veteran) runs the company; Christen runs marketing. Earlier "Christen runs the customer side/phone" was an orchestrator guess and was wrong.
- 2026-08-25 — **Price is not the objection. Capacity is.** 50 cancellation notes pulled from BookingKoala: our own availability = 12 (24%), price = 1 (and that one was a card-preauth misunderstanding). Quality/conduct = 3. Write against "can you actually fit me in," never against "you're expensive." Full data: `campaigns/icp-verification/cancellation-notes-2026.md`. [verified]
- 2026-08-25 — **The active book is monthly-dominant** (11 monthly vs 5 biweekly of 19 recurring), but biweekly has earned more lifetime revenue ($106.8K vs $96.4K) on fewer than half the customers. Open question for Todd: is monthly what this market buys, or an un-pushed upgrade? [verified]
- 2026-08-25 — **A 6-customer win-back list exists** ("I will be back", named and contactable) and nobody is working it. [verified]
- 2026-08-25 — **Acquisition source is untracked.** BookingKoala does not capture it and the Referrals report is all zeros (the referral program is unused). Any "where customers come from" claim is guesswork until GA4/PostHog or a booking-form question fills the gap. [verified]
