## Visual Style

- **Photography:** Warm, natural, real-home interiors in soft daylight. Lived-in, not staged.
  Approachable over aspirational — the brand is neighborly, not a design magazine.
- **Graphic style:** Type-led with clear negative space. Peach carries the emphasis: a brushstroke
  underline, a rule, an icon, a single highlighted word.
- **Mood:** Warm, friendly, trustworthy, capable. "Life is messy. We've got this." Reads like a
  neighbor who is genuinely good at this — not a franchise, and not a luxury boutique.
- **Texture:** Clean and soft. Gentle rounded corners and soft shadows match the live site
  (`rounded-2xl`, `shadow-lg`). Peach→apricot gradients are on-brand **for CTA surfaces**; avoid
  gradients as decorative backgrounds.

### What to avoid (prompt guardrails)
- **Navy / cream / brass** — the retired wrong palette. Actively exclude.
- Aqua/teal "cleaning brand" gradient (MaidPro's look)
- Stock-photo cleaners in matching polos with feather dusters
- Sparkles, twinkles, magic dust
- Neon or candy-bright color
- Cartoon bubbles, motion lines, comic effects
- All-caps slab headlines stacked with exclamation points
- Photoshopped before/after splits with a diagonal wipe
- Emoji-heavy graphics
- Three-people-in-a-row team photos against a white wall
- "Trusted by" logo bars when there are no real logos to show
- "Luxury" framing — not the general brand position (see `CLAUDE.md` claim guardrails)

## Claim guardrails for creative

Visual assets are subject to the same rules as site copy (`CLAUDE.md`):

- Pull every price/stat from `src/data/claims.ts`. Never invent a number for a graphic.
- Never render retired prices: $99, $119, $129, $135, $149, $175, $176, $225, $275, $400.
- No emergency or same-day cleaning claims.
- No clinical/medical compliance, OSHA, or bloodborne-pathogen claims.
- "1,500+" means **customers served**, never "cleanings completed."
- **MaidPro collision flag:** do not lead Huntsville/Shoals creative with veteran-owned,
  women-owned, or a numbered checklist — MaidPro Huntsville/Shoals uses identical claims.

## Locked style principles

**Color treatment:** Light ground (`#E5E5EA` or white), charcoal text (`#333333`), peach accent
(`#FFA985`) as the one colored element. Peach→apricot (`#FFC67D`) gradient permitted on CTA
surfaces only. Never more than three colors per composition.

**Lighting (photo assets):** Soft natural daylight, 4500K–5200K. Warm, gentle shadows. No flash,
no studio strobe, no harsh side-light.

**Composition:** Generous margin (8–10% all sides). One focal point. White space is the design.

**Technical:** 1080×1080 square (IG feed/carousel), 1080×1350 4:5 (FB/IG hero, lead-magnet covers),
1080×1920 9:16 (Reels/Stories), 1200×675 16:9 (FB link headers). PNG export, 72 DPI.
