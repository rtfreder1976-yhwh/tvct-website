# Email 4: Honest Close

## Subject Line Variants

### A: "Last note about your quote" — recommended
States exactly what it is. "Last" earns the open honestly — it's true, and the finality itself is the hook. No manufactured urgency required.

### B: "No countdown timer on this one"
The anti-scarcity subject: says out loud what every competitor's Day-9 email is doing and refuses it. Bold, dry, very on-brand — the Liquid Death "break the category convention" borrow in one line.

### C: "still deciding?"
Two words, lowercase, reads like a text from someone you know. Highest reply rate; some readers will answer the question directly, which is the best possible outcome.

**Recommended A/B test:** B vs C
**Reason:** The finale is the safest place to test the boldest subject against the most personal one. Either result teaches us more than A would.

## Send Timing
Day 9 at 6:30 PM CT — evening, when household decisions actually get made. Nine days gives the comparison shopping time to finish; a final touch later than this reads as nagging.

## Email Copy

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

This is the last email about your quote — four feels like enough.

Two honest things before we leave you alone:

There's no countdown clock on this. We don't do expiring offers, and the price won't jump because a timer ran out. The real constraint is the calendar — we usually book 2-3 days out, sometimes further.

And if the answer is no, or not yet, that's genuinely fine. We're not the right cleaning company for everyone. If you want someone there today, or a cleaner who'll work off your list instead of our checklist, we're honestly not it — and we'd rather you find the right fit than feel pestered into us.

If the answer is yes: call or text (256) 826-1100 and we'll take it from there.

— Todd
The Valley Clean Team
Life is messy. We've got this.
