# Email 2: What the Number Buys

## Subject Line Variants

### A: "What that number actually pays for" — recommended
Direct continuation of Email 1 — "that number" assumes the shared context of their quote, which personalizes without a merge field. Curiosity plus relevance.

### B: "We're not the cheapest. That's intentional."
The brand's flagship pricing line as a subject. Bold play: it says out loud what competitors dodge, which is exactly the Oatly-style honesty the brief borrows. Will polarize price-first shoppers — which is qualification, not loss.

### C: "the part of the quote you can't see"
Personal, lowercase, mild curiosity gap. Softest entry for readers who found B confrontational.

**Recommended A/B test:** A vs B
**Reason:** Directly tests whether leading with honesty-about-price helps or hurts with quote-stage leads. If B holds open rates, the not-the-cheapest frame can move earlier in future copy.

Day 2 at 8:00 AM CT — the comparison-shopping window. Dual-income households check email during the morning routine; day 2 is when the other quotes have arrived and the decision is actively being weighed.
