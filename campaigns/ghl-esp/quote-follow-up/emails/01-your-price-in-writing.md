# Email 1: Your Price, In Writing

## Subject Line Variants

### A: "Your exact price from The Valley Clean Team" — recommended
Direct benefit, matches the expectation set on the call or the quote request. Named sender + "exact price" is the brand's own proof language. Safest open for a Day 0 recap.

### B: "The number we quote is the number we bill"
The signature held-price line as a subject. Bolder — no company name, reads like a claim worth checking. Stands out against every "Your Quote from XYZ Cleaning!" competitor email in the same inbox.

### C: "your price, in writing"
Lowercase, five words, feels like a personal note from Todd rather than an automation. Highest trust signal, lowest information.

**Recommended A/B test:** A vs B
**Reason:** Tests whether recipients respond to plain clarity or to the held-price claim itself. If B wins, the transparency proof carries more weight than we're giving it — useful for every future subject line.

## Preview Text
"So you're not trying to remember a phone call."

## Send Timing
Day 0 — immediately after the quote goes out (written-quote path) or same day as the phone quote (didn't-book path). The recap has to land while the number is still fresh; any delay and a competitor's follow-up gets there first.

## Email Copy

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Thanks for asking us for a price. Here it is in writing, so you're not trying to remember a phone call.

Your quote is the number you'll see on the bill. It moves in exactly two situations, both yours: you add something to the job, or the home details we quoted from need correcting — square footage, bedrooms, baths, pets. Either way, you'll hear from us before anything changes, not after.

What it covers: our 44-item checklist, run the same way every visit, by cleaners who are background-checked and covered by $2 million in liability insurance.

When you're ready, call or text (256) 826-1100 and we'll find you a spot. We usually book 2-3 days out.

Still comparing companies? That's fine. Take your time.

— Todd
The Valley Clean Team
Life is messy. We've got this.
