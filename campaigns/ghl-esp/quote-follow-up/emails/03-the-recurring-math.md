# Email 3: The Recurring Math

## Subject Line Variants

### A: "If you're thinking about regular cleaning" — recommended
Plain relevance filter — self-selects the readers this email is for. Honest about its own content, which fits the brand better than manufactured curiosity.

### B: "The biweekly math"
Three words, specific, slightly unexpected. "Math" promises a concrete number rather than a pitch. Bold in its brevity; passes Christen's plain-word test where "arithmetic" failed.

### C: "one thing most people don't ask about"
Personal curiosity gap, lowercase. Mirrors the email's opening line so the subject and body feel continuous.

**Recommended A/B test:** A vs C
**Reason:** Tests stated-topic subjects against soft-curiosity subjects on the same audience. The winner sets the default pattern for future value emails in this list.

## Preview Text

## Send Timing
Day 5 at 7:30 AM CT — mid-consideration. By now the one-time price has been weighed; the recurring frame either unsticks a stalled decision or upgrades it. Morning send, before the day's decisions pile up.
