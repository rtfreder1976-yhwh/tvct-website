# Email 3: The Easy No

## Subject Line Variants

### A: "Last note about this"
Standard finality signal, states the email's job honestly. Safe, and it earns the open because it is true.

### B: "Monthly is a fine answer" — recommended
Unusual for an upgrade sequence: the subject line gives them permission to decline before they open. On a list of eleven existing customers, protecting the relationship is worth more than squeezing a conversion, and this framing does that while still prompting the open.

### C: "closing this out"
Lowercase, administrative, zero pressure. Good, but B does the same work while explicitly removing the guilt.

**Recommended A/B test:** B vs A
**Reason:** Tests whether permission-to-decline outperforms standard finality on existing customers. If B wins, that framing belongs in every retention email we write.
