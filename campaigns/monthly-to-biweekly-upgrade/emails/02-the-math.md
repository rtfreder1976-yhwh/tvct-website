# Email 2: The Math

## Subject Line Variants

### A: "25% instead of 15%" — recommended
Two numbers, no adjectives, under 20 characters. Anyone who read email 1 knows exactly what this refers to; anyone who did not will open out of curiosity. Digits in a subject line stand out in a text-heavy inbox.

### B: "The part people assume wrong"
Curiosity gap aimed at the specific misconception (that more frequent means proportionally more expensive). Bolder, but vaguer — it works only if the body pays it off immediately, which it does.

### C: "the numbers, if it helps"
Lowercase, unpushy, explicitly optional. The most respectful option for an existing customer who may have already decided no.

**Recommended A/B test:** A vs B
**Reason:** Tests whether stating the offer plainly beats the curiosity approach on a most-aware audience. With existing customers, plainness usually wins — this checks that assumption.
