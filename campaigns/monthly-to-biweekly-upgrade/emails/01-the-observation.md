# Email 1: The Observation

## Subject Line Variants

### A: "Something we notice on monthly cleans" — recommended
Frames the email as an observation from people who do this professionally, not a pitch. "We notice" is the expert-friend register, and it names the topic honestly so nobody feels tricked into opening a sales email.

### B: "Week three is where it turns"
Specific and slightly intriguing — a claim that invites the question "what turns?" Bolder, and it puts the mechanism in the subject rather than the topic.

### C: "a thought about your schedule"
Lowercase, low-key, reads like a note from someone who knows them. Softest option; lowest risk of feeling like marketing to an existing customer.

**Recommended A/B test:** A vs C
**Reason:** Tests professional-observation framing against personal-note framing on a list of existing customers. On an eleven-person list the result is directional at best, but it tells us how these customers prefer to be approached about money.

## Preview Text
"Not a complaint about your house. An observation about calendars."

## Send Timing
Day 0, Tuesday-Thursday 7:30 AM CT. Morning routine window for dual-income households (74% of real traffic is mobile). Avoid sending near their actual service date, so it reads as a general note rather than a comment on the visit that just happened.

## Email Copy

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Something we notice, and it has nothing to do with how anyone keeps their house.

On a monthly schedule, about three weeks in, dust has had time to settle back onto everything we wiped. Soap film returns to the shower glass. The kitchen floor picks up the traffic of a full month. None of that is unusual — it is just what time does to a house with people living in it.

What it means practically is that part of every monthly visit gets spent catching back up, rather than staying ahead. Same 44-item checklist, but more of it goes to recovering ground.

At every two weeks, that does not happen. The house never gets far enough from baseline to need catching up, so the same visit goes further.

Two other things worth knowing, since they surprise people:

Biweekly is a bigger discount than monthly — 25% off our regular rate instead of 15%. And a biweekly slot is a standing spot on the schedule, which matters more than it used to; our calendar has been tight this year.

If you want to try it, reply to this or call (256) 826-1100 and we will move your next visit. If monthly is working for you, that is genuinely fine and you can ignore this.

— Todd
The Valley Clean Team
