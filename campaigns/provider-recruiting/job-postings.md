## Publishing note

These postings are written for independent cleaning providers, not W-2 employees. They are transparent about the business-to-business relationship and avoid promising guaranteed hours, shifts, benefits, or employment.

Compensation is advertised as an estimated hourly equivalent because providers receive flat-rate pay per completed job rather than hourly wages:

> **Typical earning equivalent: $25–$45 per hour**, based on experience, job scope, and completion time. Each job's flat-rate provider pay is shown before acceptance. Actual effective hourly earnings vary by assignment and provider efficiency.

## Florence and The Shoals, Alabama

### Title

Independent House Cleaning Provider — Florence & The Shoals

### Full posting

The Valley Clean Team is expanding its network of reliable independent cleaning providers in Florence and the Shoals area, including Muscle Shoals, Sheffield, Tuscumbia, Killen, and nearby communities.

This opportunity is a good fit for an experienced cleaner or small cleaning business that already knows how to deliver professional residential cleaning and wants access to additional jobs without giving up control of its own business.

### How the opportunity works

- Available cleaning jobs are matched to your service area and availability.
- You decide whether to accept an offered job.
- Job scope and flat-rate provider pay are shared before acceptance.
- Typical earning equivalent is $25–$45 per hour based on experience, job scope, completion time, and provider efficiency.
- You use your own reliable transportation, professional supplies, and equipment.
- This is an independent-contractor/provider opportunity; jobs and earnings are not guaranteed.

### Services may include

- Recurring residential cleaning
- Deep cleaning
- Move-in and move-out cleaning
- Short-term-rental and turnover cleaning

### We are looking for providers who

- Have professional residential-cleaning experience
- Communicate promptly and professionally
- Have reliable transportation and a smartphone
- Have their own professional cleaning supplies and equipment
- Can accurately manage their availability
- Take ownership of quality and customer satisfaction
- Are comfortable documenting completed work with before-and-after photos
- Can complete the required screening and onboarding process
- Carry, or are willing and able to obtain, appropriate general liability insurance before regular assignments

Qualified applicants will complete a short application, an initial screening, a video interview, and provider onboarding. Providers who successfully complete the process may be added to our ready-provider network.
