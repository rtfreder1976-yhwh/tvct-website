# Move-Out Cleaning in Athens, AL: The Deposit-Back Checklist (2026)

Move-out cleaning in Athens starts at $199 for an apartment, and the whole point of it is money — your deposit. Your Athens landlord has the legal right to deduct cleaning costs from what you get back, and over the years landlords here have gotten thorough about it. The oven interior. The refrigerator drip pan. The window tracks that haven't been touched since you moved in. This guide is the room-by-room list they actually inspect, what it costs to do it right in Limestone County, and the seven spots that quietly cost renters their deposit even after a full weekend of scrubbing.

This isn't a generic national checklist. We've cleaned move-outs in Athens hundreds of times — apartment complexes on US-72, single-family rentals in established neighborhoods, newer townhomes near the Hwy 31 corridor. The list below is the one that comes from doing the work.

## Why a move-out clean is different from a regular clean

A regular clean maintains a home you're living in. A move-out clean is graded — by someone whose job is to find reasons to keep part of your deposit.

That changes everything. When the furniture is gone, the inspector sees what was hidden: the rectangle of grime where the couch sat, the baseboards behind the bed, the dust on top of the door frames, the cabinet shelves you never wiped because they were full. Move-out cleaning targets exactly those spots. It's more detailed than a standard clean, which is why it's priced by the job, not the hour, and why it takes longer than you'd expect for an empty house.

Here's the honest framing most cleaning companies won't give you: **the goal isn't a clean house. It's a passed inspection.** Those aren't always the same thing, and knowing the difference is how you keep your money.

## The Athens move-out cleaning checklist, room by room

Print this. Work it top to bottom. The order matters — dust falls, so you clean high before you clean floors.

**Kitchen (where most deposits are won or lost)**
- Inside the oven, including the door glass and the racks
- Inside the refrigerator — shelves, drawers, and the drip pan underneath
- Stovetop, including under the drip pans or burner grates
- Range hood and filter
- Inside and outside of every cabinet and drawer — every shelf, not just the reachable ones
- Countertops, backsplash, and sink (polish the faucet — inspectors notice)
- Dishwasher interior and door gasket

**Bathrooms**
- Grout and caulk lines in the shower and tub (mildew here is a classic deduction)
- Shower door tracks and glass
- Toilet, base, and the floor behind it
- Exhaust fan cover (dust builds up and it's on every inspection list)
- Mirror, counter, sink, and cabinet interiors
- Floor, scrubbed to the edges

**Living areas and bedrooms**
- Baseboards — all of them, including the corners where dust and pet hair collect
- Ceiling fans and light fixtures (blades hold a surprising amount of dust)
- Blinds, wiped slat by slat
- Window interiors, sills, and tracks
- Door frames and the tops of doors
- Closets — emptied, shelves wiped, floor cleaned
- Outlet covers and light switches

**Floors (this is lease-dependent — read yours)**
- Carpet: many Athens apartment leases require *professional* carpet cleaning, not vacuuming. If yours does and you skip it, expect a deduction.
- Hardwood and LVP: swept and mopped to the edges of the room
- Tile: grout lines included, especially in older units near Athens State where the grout has absorbed years of use

Different properties come with different challenges. Older homes and apartments near Athens State have tile grout that needs specific techniques. Newer construction along Highway 31 has modern finishes that need the right products, not just elbow grease. Rural Limestone County properties often have mudrooms and garage-access areas that fall outside a standard interior clean.

## What Athens property managers actually check

Not every inch. Inspectors have a routine, and they hit the same high-value spots every time because those are where they can defend a deduction. From the move-outs we've done across Limestone County, the walk-through almost always includes:

- **The oven and fridge**, opened and inspected inside — the two most-deducted items in any rental
- **Window tracks and sills**, run with a finger
- **Bathroom grout and caulk**, checked for mildew and staining
- **Baseboards and corners**, where DIY cleans run out of patience
- **Floors to the edges** — the middle of the room always gets cleaned; the perimeter is where people quit
- **The smell test** — pet odor or smoke is subjective, but it drives deductions

If your landlord or property manager gives you a specific move-out checklist, that's a gift. Use it as your exact to-do list, and hand it to whoever cleans so nothing gets missed.

## 7 spots that void your deposit even if you cleaned

These are the ones that get people. Every one of them is a common deduction, and every one is easy to miss if you're cleaning the way you clean a home you live in:

1. **Stovetop drip pans.** People clean the surface and forget what's underneath. Lift them.
2. **Refrigerator drip pan and gasket.** The rubber seal around the door traps grime, and the pan underneath is out of sight.
3. **Dryer lint trap and vent area.** Frequently on apartment checklists, almost never cleaned.
4. **Blinds.** Wiped slat by slat, not just dusted. Dusty blinds read as "didn't really clean."
5. **Bathroom grout and caulk.** Slow mildew buildup that a quick wipe won't touch.
6. **Inside the oven.** The single most-deducted item, and the one people most want to skip.
7. **Smell.** Pet odor, smoke, or a musty vacant-house smell. Subjective, but it colors the whole inspection.

Miss one or two of these and an otherwise-clean unit can still cost you.

## What a "professional move-out clean" should guarantee

Not all move-out cleaning is equal, and the price isn't the thing that separates them. Before you book anyone, ask for three things:

- **A re-clean guarantee.** If the property manager finds a cleaning issue, will they come back and fix it, free? Our Athens move-out guarantee is exactly this: if your property manager flags something we were responsible for, call within 72 hours and we return at no charge. No debate.
- **A written, flat quote.** You should know the number before the work starts — no hourly meter running, no surprise at the end.
- **Insurance and a real team.** Background-checked, insured, bonded. If something breaks in an empty unit during a clean, that matters.

The re-clean guarantee is the one that actually protects your deposit, because it means the risk of a rejected clean isn't yours anymore.

## When to book your move-out clean

Timing trips people up. The clean has to happen **after** you've moved everything out but **before** the final walk-through — and that window is often tight.

- **Best case:** clean the day after your furniture is gone, a day or two before the inspection. That leaves room to fix anything.
- **Common case:** same-day, right before you hand over the keys. Workable, but no margin.
- **Avoid:** cleaning around furniture and boxes. You'll miss exactly the spots the inspector checks — the ones that were under your stuff.

Most Athens leases time the final walk-through for the last day of the month, so book early — end-of-month turnover is the busiest window for every cleaner in Limestone County.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does move-out cleaning cost in Athens, AL?

Move-out cleaning in Athens starts at $199 for apartments and 1-2 bedroom homes. An average 3-bedroom runs $225-$299, and larger or rougher units get a custom quote. Gig apps advertise ~$19-$22/hour, but a move-out is priced by the job, and those rates come without insurance or a re-clean guarantee.

### What's included in a move-out cleaning?

Everything a landlord inspects on an empty unit: inside the oven and refrigerator, cabinet and drawer interiors, window tracks and sills, baseboards, door frames, light fixtures and fans, bathroom grout and caulk, and floors cleaned to the edges. It's deliberately more detailed than a regular clean.

### Will my landlord refund the deposit if I clean myself?

Sometimes. Alabama landlords can deduct reasonable cleaning costs, so a DIY clean only helps if it meets the inspector's standard. Most self-cleans lose the deposit on a few predictable spots — oven, fridge drip pan, window tracks, grout. Photograph your work in case of a dispute.

### How long does move-out cleaning take?

A 3-bedroom Athens home typically takes 4-6 hours done thoroughly. Apartments run 3-4 hours; larger or rough units, 6-8. An empty home is quicker to move through, but the extra detail evens it out.

### Do I need to clean the carpets when moving out of an apartment?

Read your lease. Many Athens apartment leases require *professional* carpet cleaning at move-out and deduct for it if it's skipped. Vacuuming doesn't count, and hard floors need mopping to the edges, not just the middle.

### Is move-out cleaning worth it?

If your deposit is bigger than the cost of the clean, usually yes — a clean from $199 that recovers a $500-$1,000 deposit pays for itself. It's less clear-cut if your deposit is small, your unit's in good shape, and you have time to clean to standard yourself.

### What's the difference between move-in and move-out cleaning?

Move-out cleaning readies an empty unit for a landlord's inspection so you get your deposit back. Move-in cleaning readies a unit for you, since you don't know how the last tenant left it. Both are detailed cleans of an empty home — the difference is who it's for.
