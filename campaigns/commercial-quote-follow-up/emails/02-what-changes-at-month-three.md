# Email 2: What Changes at Month Three

## Subject Line Variants

### A: "What usually changes around month three" — recommended
Specific and slightly ominous without being negative. Any facility manager who has managed a cleaning vendor knows exactly what this means before opening — recognition is the hook.

### B: "The cleaning company was great. Then it wasn't."
The buyer's own experience, stated as a two-sentence story. Bolder and more memorable; the risk is that it reads as a competitor swipe rather than an industry observation.

### C: "Why facility cleaning slips"
Plainest of the three, positions the email as analysis rather than sales. Best for a technically-minded reader; lowest emotional pull.

**Recommended A/B test:** A vs B
**Reason:** Tests understated recognition against the more vivid retelling. Whichever wins tells us how direct this audience wants us to be about competitor failure.

## Preview Text
"Nobody quotes you the third month. That's the one that matters."

## Send Timing
Day 4, Tuesday-Thursday 9:00 AM CT. Far enough from the quote to not read as pressure, early enough to land while comparison is still active.

## Email Copy

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Most cleaning vendors look identical on paper. The difference shows up around month three.

The first few visits are usually good — the crew is new, someone is paying attention, and the walkthrough is fresh in everyone's mind. Then the assigned cleaner changes. Then a different person covers a sick day. Nobody hands off what the last person knew about your building, and slowly the baseboards stop getting done and the break room starts getting a lighter pass than it used to.

You notice in month four. By then you are having an awkward conversation about something you already paid for.

The way we deal with this is unglamorous: a written checklist for your building, worked in the same order every visit. What gets cleaned does not depend on who is on the crew that night or what they happen to remember. New person, covered shift, holiday week — same list, same order.

That is also why we can tell you what a visit includes before you sign, rather than after you complain.

If it would help to see the actual checklist we would run for your space, reply and I will send it.
