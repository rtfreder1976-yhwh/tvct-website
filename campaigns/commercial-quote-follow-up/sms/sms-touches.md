# SMS — Commercial Quote Follow-Up

_Created 2026-08-25. Status: DRAFT. GHL SMS confirmed live by Todd 2026-08-25._

## ⚠️ Read this before using commercial SMS at all

Texting a **business contact** is not the same as texting a homeowner who asked for a house-cleaning quote:

- The number on an Apollo-enriched record is often a **direct office line or a personal mobile the person did not hand us**. Enrichment is not consent.
- A cold text to a practice manager reads as intrusive in a way an email does not, and it can cost the deal outright.
- **Only text a commercial contact who has already replied to us, or who gave the number during a call or walkthrough.**

**Rule: no SMS to the Apollo medical batch #4 until they have engaged first.** These messages are for warm commercial conversations, not cold outreach. If in doubt, do not text — the 2-business-hour written quote is the promise, and email honors it.

## Rules these obey

- Under 300 characters. Signed **Todd Frederickson** (full name — commercial register, unlike residential's first-name-only).
- Measured. No wit, no asides, no exclamation marks anywhere in this file.
- Business hours only, 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM CT, Tuesday-Thursday preferred. Never evenings or weekends to a business contact.
- AL: (256) 826-1100. TN: (615) 510-1427.
- No numeric commercial pricing, ever. Commercial is custom-quoted from three inputs.

## The touches

One, plus a conditional. Commercial decisions run on committees and budget cycles; texting into that repeatedly is noise.

---

### SMS 1 — Day 5 (only if the contact has replied at least once)

**Send:** Day 5, Tue-Thu, 10:00 AM CT.

> Hi {{contact.first_name}} — Todd Frederickson, The Valley Clean Team. Following up on the cleaning quote we sent. Happy to send the certificate of insurance or adjust the scope if that helps. (256) 826-1100

**205 characters.** Purpose: move the paperwork forward. The COI offer is the single most useful thing you can put in front of a facility manager mid-procurement, because it is the thing their own process will demand next.

---

### SMS 2 — Day 22, conditional close (only after email 4 gets no reply)

**Send:** Day 22, Tue-Thu, 10:00 AM CT. Last contact of any kind.

> Hi {{contact.first_name}} — closing the file on the cleaning quote unless you would like it kept open. A yes, a no, or a month to revisit are all useful answers. — Todd Frederickson, The Valley Clean Team

**203 characters.** Purpose: the same three-option close as email 4, in the channel a busy manager actually answers from. Mirrors the email deliberately so it reads as consistent rather than as a new pitch.
