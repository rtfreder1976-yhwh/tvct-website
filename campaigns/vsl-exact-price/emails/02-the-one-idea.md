# Email 2: The One Idea

## Subject Line Variants

### A: "The number we quote is the number we bill" — recommended
The brand's signature proof line, stated flat. Reads as a claim worth verifying, which is exactly the reaction we want.

### B: "Two exceptions, both of them yours"
Curiosity plus fairness in six words. Bolder — it makes no sense until opened, which is the risk and the appeal.

### C: "in case you didn't watch it"
Honest, self-aware, lowercase. Acknowledges reality (most won't have watched) without guilt-tripping.

**Recommended A/B test:** A vs C
**Reason:** Tests the proof-claim against plain self-awareness. C is the more human option and will tell us how much the list rewards that register.

## Email Copy

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Most of the video comes down to one sentence: the number we quote is the number we bill.

That price moves in exactly two situations, and both of them are yours. You add something to the job. Or the details we quoted from need correcting — square footage, bedrooms, baths, pets. Either way, you'll hear from us before anything changes, not after.

What it doesn't move for: how the house looks when we get there. That's our problem to plan for, not yours to pay for.

Call (256) 826-1100 with your square footage and you'll have your number before you hang up.

— Todd
The Valley Clean Team
