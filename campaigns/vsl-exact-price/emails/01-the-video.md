# Email 1: The Video

## Subject Line Variants

### A: "Three minutes on why our price is what it is" — recommended
Names the runtime up front, which is the single biggest objection to pressing play. Specific and honest about what it is.

### B: "I recorded the pricing explanation"
Personal and slightly unexpected — a cleaning company owner recording himself explaining pricing is not what the inbox expects. Works best on the Todd-on-camera version.

### C: "the part nobody explains"
Lowercase curiosity gap. Softest, least informative; best if A and B both underperform.

**Recommended A/B test:** A vs B
**Reason:** Tests whether naming the runtime (removes friction) beats naming the person (adds trust). Useful for every future video email.
