# Email 3: Honest Close

## Subject Line Variants

### A: "We're not the right company for everyone" — recommended
The Patagonia move as a subject line. Disarming in an inbox full of companies insisting they're perfect for everybody, and it's the brand's own approved sentence.

### B: "Who we tell to hire someone else"
Same idea, more curiosity, slightly more clever. Risk: cleverness is the failure mode the brief warns about.

### C: "last one from me"
Plain, personal, finite. Signals the sequence is ending, which earns a final open honestly.

**Recommended A/B test:** A vs C
**Reason:** Tests the brand's confident-qualifier voice against pure plainness on the final touch. Whichever wins should set the pattern for future closes.

## Email Copy

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Last one from me, and it's the part of the video people mention most.

We'd love to clean for everybody. We can't.

If you need someone there today, that's not us — we usually book two or three days out.

And if you want a cleaner who'll work off your list instead of ours, room by room, we're honestly not it either. We run a system, and the system is the whole product. Some people want the other thing, and that's a completely fair thing to want.

If either of those is you, there are good companies around here that work that way. I'd rather you find one than be disappointed in us.

If it isn't — call (256) 826-1100 and we'll get you your number.

— Todd
The Valley Clean Team
Life is messy. We've got this.
