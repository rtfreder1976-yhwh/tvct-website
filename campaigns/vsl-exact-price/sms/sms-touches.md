## Rules

- Under 300 characters. First name. Signed **Todd**.
- The link is the payload. Do not decorate it — a text that hypes a video reads as spam, and this audience is already skeptical of cleaning-company marketing.
- No exclamation marks, no emoji.
- AL: (256) 826-1100. TN: (615) 510-1427.
- 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM in the contact's local time.

## The touches

### SMS 1 — Day 1 (the day after the video email)

**Send:** Day 1, 9:00 AM CT.

> Hi {{contact.first_name}} — Todd with The Valley Clean Team. Sent you a short video yesterday on how our pricing actually works, in case it landed in spam: [VIDEO LINK] About 3 minutes. — Todd

**~185 characters + link.** Purpose: the same spam-rescue job as the residential Day 1 text. Video emails are more likely than plain text to be filtered, so this matters more here than elsewhere.

### SMS 2 — Day 8 (after the honest-close email)

**Send:** Day 8, 5:30 PM CT. Final contact.

> Hi {{contact.first_name}} — last note from me. If you want your exact price, call (256) 826-1100 with your square footage and you will have it before you hang up. If not, no hard feelings. — Todd

**191 characters.** Purpose: closes on the phone number rather than the video. By day 8 the video has either done its job or it has not; the ask should be the actual conversion action.

## Note on the pairing

SMS 1 carries the link; SMS 2 deliberately does not. Sending the same link twice implies the first send failed, which trains people to ignore both.
