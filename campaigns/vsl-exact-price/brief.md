# Campaign: The Exact Price VSL

_Created 2026-08-25 by /start-here + /direct-response-copy. Status: ALL DRAFT — script awaiting Todd/Christen review. Nothing produced, nothing published._

## Why this exists

The measured problem is **CTR and trust, not traffic**: ~43K search impressions/month, positions 4-10 converting at 0.29%, near-zero phone clicks ([[ctr-not-position-is-the-problem]]). A video is a trust instrument — it does a job text on a page cannot, which is let a prospect judge a person before they call.

The material is unusually good for this: a veteran-owned company whose actual differentiator (a firm price on the phone, held) is *easier to demonstrate out loud than to argue in writing*.

## What this is NOT

Not a classic direct-response VSL. No 18-minute runtime, no big-promise open, no stacked offer, no countdown, no "click below right now." That register is banned by `brand/brand-brief.md` and would be absurd for a $200 local service decision.

This is **the honest explanation, on camera**: the same argument the homepage hero makes, delivered by a person, in 3-4 minutes.

## Format decision (Todd, 2026-08-25)

Todd on camera reading the script; an **AI avatar** is the preferred production route if quality allows.

> ⚠️ **Flagged risk — Todd's call.** The script's persuasive force is "a real person telling you the truth about money." If a viewer identifies the presenter as synthetic while it is *presented as Todd*, the transparency argument inverts: a company preaching honesty used a fake founder. Two safe routes:
> 1. **Avatar as unattributed narrator** — no claimed identity, voiceover register, b-roll heavy. Honest because it never pretends to be a specific person.
> 2. **Todd on camera** for the version that says "I'm Todd."
>
> Recommended path: produce the avatar version FIRST as a cheap test of whether the script lands, then film Todd for the version that ships if it does. The script below is written to work either way — the bracketed identity lines are the only thing that changes.

## Placement (both)

1. **On-site**: `/pricing` and homepage, above or beside the existing hero. Targets existing traffic that is already arriving and not calling.
2. **Email**: the hook for a 3-email sequence to quote leads who have not booked. Hosted on the connected YouTube channel (`@thevalleycleanteamal`), unlisted or public.

## Runtime + structure

3:30-4:00. Five beats, no fat:

| Beat | Seconds | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. The honest open | 0:00-0:25 | Say the unexpected thing: we're not the cheapest. Earn the next 30 seconds. |
| 2. Why the price is the price | 0:25-1:30 | The mechanism: sq ft + type of cleaning, read off a rate card, held. |
| 3. What the number buys | 1:30-2:40 | 44-item checklist, background checks, $2M, the 24-hour re-clean. |
| 4. Who we're not for | 2:40-3:10 | The Patagonia move. Qualifies out, which makes the rest credible. |
| 5. The ask | 3:10-3:40 | One action: call, say your square footage, get the number. |

## Production notes (per `brand/creative-kit.md`)

- Palette: light ground, charcoal text, **one** peach `#FFA985` element per composition. No navy, no cream, no gold-foil.
- On-screen text: Playfair Display 700 for the one big line; Inter for everything else. Sentence case. Digits always. **No exclamation marks.**
- B-roll should be *specific*, never luxury props: a checklist line item, one particular before/after spot, the phone with the real number on it. Never a chandelier or marble foyer.
- Lower third: "Todd Frederickson · The Valley Clean Team" ONLY on the version Todd actually delivers.
- Captions burned in — most views will be muted on mobile (74% of real AL/TN traffic is mobile per `brand/audience.md`).
- End card: the phone number, big, on peach. AL (256) 826-1100. A TN cut swaps (615) 510-1427.

## Claims used (all from `src/data/claims.ts` at write time)

Regular from $200 · deep from $276 · 44-item checklist · $2M liability · background checks · re-clean reported within 24 hours · 4.9 from 148 Google reviews · 1,500+ customers served · weekly 30% / biweekly 25% / monthly 15% · two price-change exceptions, both the customer's.

**Not used, deliberately:** bonded (false), workers' comp (unverified), same-day, W-2/employee framing (TVCT uses subcontractors), any invented price band.
