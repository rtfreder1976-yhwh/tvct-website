# VSL Distribution Map — where "The Exact Price" video gets used

_Created 2026-08-25. The video is one asset; this is every place it earns its keep. Todd's direction: the VSL should not sit in its own campaign — it should be woven into the sequences that already exist._

## The placeholder

Every reference below uses **`[VIDEO LINK]`** as a literal placeholder. Once the video is produced and hosted on the connected YouTube channel (`@thevalleycleanteamal`), one find-and-replace across `campaigns/` fills them all in.

**Do not send any sequence containing `[VIDEO LINK]`.** Search for that string before pushing anything to GHL — it is the single check that prevents a broken link reaching a customer.

## Where it goes

| Campaign | Placement | Why it fits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Quote follow-up** (residential) | Email 2, as an optional opener line | They already have their price; the video explains *why* it holds. Reinforces the exact-price proof at the moment they're comparing. |
| **Commercial quote follow-up** | Email 2, adapted | Facility managers get the same "why the number holds" argument. Register stays measured — no "check out our video." |
| **Monthly → biweekly upgrade** | **Not used** | These are existing customers who have already lived the service. Sending them a prospect-facing explainer is condescending. Deliberate omission. |
| **VSL sequence** (its own 3 emails) | The whole point | Still exists as a standalone nurture for leads who never got a quote. |
| **Social batch** | GBP + Facebook + Instagram cuts | The atomizer can pull 3-5 short cuts from the script's five beats. Not yet written — needs the video first. |
| **Site** | `/pricing` + homepage embed | Targets the CTR problem directly: existing traffic that arrives and doesn't call. |
| **SMS** | One touch per sequence | See `sms/` in each campaign. Short link only, never a paragraph. |

## Why it is NOT everywhere

Three deliberate omissions, so nobody "fixes" them later:

1. **Not in any Day 0 email.** The first message delivers the quote. Adding a video to it splits the CTA and delays the number, which is the opposite of the brand promise.
2. **Not in the upgrade sequence.** Existing customers do not need to be sold the company they already pay.
3. **Not in the honest-close emails.** Those ask for a decision. A video is a new thing to consume, which reads as a stall at the exact moment we are asking someone to commit.

## Production dependency

Nothing above works until the video exists. The blocking item is Todd recording the script in `script.md` (~3:40). Everything else — hosting, embedding, find-replace — is minutes of work after that.
