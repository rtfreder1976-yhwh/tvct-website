# Threads — Mini-Thread (provider-first philosophy)

**Post 1 of 4**

The cleaning industry has a turnover problem, and most of the fixes people try are about hiring.

We think it's about the deal. Here's how our team actually works. 🧵

---

**Post 2 of 4**

With us it's a 50/50 split. Providers keep control of their own schedule and their own jobs.

They don't work for us — we work with them.

It's a different arrangement than most people expect in this industry.

---

**Post 3 of 4**

So what do we do?

The boring stuff. Marketing. Scheduling. Client management. The difficult conversations. Getting everyone paid.

And we still do the cleaning ourselves too. That's part of the deal.

---

**Post 4 of 4**

The thing we hire on isn't years of experience. It's heart.

One of our providers cleans for a man with dementia and told us, "my heart is full for helping this man."

That's the person we're looking for.
