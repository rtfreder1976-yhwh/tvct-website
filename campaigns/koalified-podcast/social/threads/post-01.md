# Threads — Post 01

We started this cleaning company on a couch.

Not a figure of speech. Todd had shattered his leg — five months off his feet. New baby at home. Christen had just been laid off. Somewhere in the middle of all that, immobile on the couch, the whole idea came together.

We launched that November. First customer in December. Under $5,000, no bank loan.

"There is no perfect time to start. We were literally forced to start."

Still not sure we'd have done it any other way.
