# Twitter/X Single 02 — The 50/50 model

---

Most cleaning companies keep ~80% of the ticket. The cleaner keeps ~20%.

We split 50/50.

"They don't work for us — we work with them."

They keep their schedule. We handle the boring stuff. Fair pay is the whole strategy.
