# Bluesky — Post 01 (the under-$5K bootstrap)

We started The Valley Clean Team for under $5,000. No bank loan, no investors.

The timing looked terrible on paper. Inside about a year we'd had a baby, Todd had shattered his leg — airlifted, five months non-weight-bearing — and Christen had been laid off from her marketing job with a 10-month-old at home.

So we built it from the couch. Todd, stuck off his feet, went deep on how people buy and build small businesses. We brainstormed the company name in a doctor's office. Launched that November. First customer in December.

"We had the internet, our brains, and time."

That was the whole starting budget. Turns out it was enough.
