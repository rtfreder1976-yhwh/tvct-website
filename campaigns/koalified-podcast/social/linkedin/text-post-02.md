# LinkedIn Text Post 02 — The Provider-First Model (leadership / culture)

First 3 lines carry the hook. Written for the operator/founder audience on LinkedIn.

---

"They don't work for us. We work with them."

That's the sentence our whole company runs on, and it's the opposite of how most of our industry operates.

We split revenue 50/50 with the provider who does the work.

We split 50/50.

Half the ticket goes to the person in the home doing the work. They keep control of their own schedule and the jobs they take. We handle the boring stuff — marketing, client management, scheduling, payment, the occasional hard conversation.

People ask why we'd give away half. Two reasons.

One: we hire for heart, and you can't retain heart on a squeeze. A while back a provider cleaned for a man with dementia and told us, "my heart is full for helping this man." That's the person you want in someone's home. You don't keep that person on 20%.

Two: quality follows fairness. We'd rather have 30 five-star reviews than 100 one-star reviews. A provider who's paid fairly does careful work — and careful work is the only marketing that compounds.

A version of the Steve Jobs line has stuck with me: hire people smarter than you, then get out of their way. Our job isn't to manage the clean. It's to remove everything that keeps a good person from doing it well.

We don't just run the business. We still do the work too.
