# LinkedIn Carousel — The Provider-First Model

Angle: the operating model most cleaning companies get wrong, told slide by slide.
Design note: plain type on a calm background. One idea per slide. Skip stock cleaning-clip-art — use real job photos or clean text slides.

Episode: [PODCAST_URL]

---

## Slide 1 (cover)

**Most cleaning companies keep 80% of what a cleaner earns.**

We keep 50%.

Here's the model, and why it changed everything. →

---

## Slide 2

The industry norm looks like this:

A cleaner does the work. The company takes roughly 80% of the ticket. The cleaner takes what's left.

We started The Valley Clean Team in November 2022 and decided not to run it that way.

---

## Slide 3

Our split is 50/50.

Same job. Same house. The person doing the work keeps half.

We split revenue 50/50 with the provider who does the work.

---

## Slide 4

The line we keep coming back to:

**"They don't work for us — we work with them."**

Providers keep control of their own schedule and the jobs they take. We don't own their time.

---

## Slide 5

So what do we actually do?

The boring stuff. Marketing. Client management. Scheduling. Payment. The hard conversations when they come up.

The provider cleans. We handle everything around the clean.

---

## Slide 6

Why give away half?

Because we hire for heart, and people with heart don't stay where they're squeezed.

One of our providers cleaned for a man with dementia and told us afterward: "my heart is full for helping this man." That's who we want.

---

## Slide 7

The math people miss:

We'd rather have 30 five-star reviews than 100 one-star reviews. Quality over quantity keeps providers and clients both.

A fairly paid provider does careful work. Careful work earns the review. The review brings the next client.

---

## Slide 8 (close)

We started this on a couch, for under $5,000, after a broken leg and a layoff.

The model was the point from day one.
