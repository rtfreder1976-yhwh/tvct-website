**Headline:** They don't work for us — we work with them.
**Body:** A 50/50 split with every provider (the industry norm is closer to 20%). They keep their own schedule and jobs. We carry the marketing, scheduling, and client side.
**Visual:** Big brass "50 / 50" numeral centered, navy headline above, grey body below.
