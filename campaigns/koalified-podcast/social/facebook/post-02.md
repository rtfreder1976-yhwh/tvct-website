# Facebook — Post 02 (the human moment)

> Distinct from anything in social-posts.md. Handles the dementia-client story with respect — the person cleaned for is never identified, the focus is on heart, not spectacle.

When people ask what we look for in a provider, they expect us to say years of experience. We don't.

We look for heart.

One of our providers cleans for an older gentleman living with dementia. After a visit, she told us something we haven't stopped thinking about:

"My heart is full for helping this man."

That's it. That's the whole hiring standard. You can teach someone a checklist. You can't teach them to care about the person whose home they're standing in.

A clean house matters. But some days the visit is about more than the house — it's about someone being looked after, treated gently, and left a little better than we found them.

We're grateful for the people on our team who show up that way. And we're grateful you let us into your homes.
