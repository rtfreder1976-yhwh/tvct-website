# Facebook — Post 03 (meet the team behind your cleaners)

> Distinct from FB 2 in social-posts.md (which explains the provider model mechanics). This one is appreciation — a thank-you to the people doing the work, meant to be shared and to make the team feel seen.

You see one or two people at your door on cleaning day. Here's who's behind them.

Thirteen providers. A few work as teams, so it's closer to seventeen hands. One office teammate, Michelle, keeping the schedule and the details straight. And the two of us — Todd and Christen — still doing the work ourselves, right alongside everyone else.

With us it's a 50/50 split, and providers keep control of their own schedule and their own jobs. They don't work for us — we work with them. We handle the parts nobody enjoys — the marketing, the scheduling, the difficult conversations, the payment — so they can do what they're good at.

We hire for one thing above all: heart. And this group has it.

To our providers: thank you. You are the reason a neighbor trusts us with their home, then tells a friend. We don't take that lightly.
