# Facebook — Post 01 (the founding story, neighbor-first)

> Distinct from FB 1 in social-posts.md (a straight episode announcement). This one is warmer, told as a neighbor talking to neighbors — roots in North Alabama, not a "we did a podcast" headline.

Both of us grew up here. Christen in the Shoals — Muscle Shoals and Tuscumbia. Todd in Decatur, where his dad worked at TVA. We lived all over — Atlanta, Vegas, Washington state — and came home to North Alabama for the small-town feel, the music history, and yes, the college football.

We didn't come home planning to start a cleaning company. Honestly, we didn't plan it at all.

In about one year we had a new baby, Todd shattered his leg — airlifted, five months off his feet — and Christen was laid off from her marketing job with a 10-month-old at home. Somewhere in the middle of all of it, stuck on that couch, the idea took shape. We brainstormed the name at a doctor's office. We launched that November, and our first customer called that December.

We started it for under $5,000. No bank loan. As Todd puts it: "We had the internet, our brains, and time."

Three and a half years later, we're a husband-and-wife team raising our girl and cleaning homes across the Valley — with a group of providers we're proud to work alongside.
