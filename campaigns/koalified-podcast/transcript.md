## Key story beats (index for asset creation)

1. **Origins** — both Alabama-born; Christen from Muscle Shoals/Tuscumbia, Todd from Decatur (dad worked at TVA). Lived all over the US (Atlanta, Vegas, WA state) before returning to North Alabama for the "small town feel," central location, music history, college football.
2. **Family of entrepreneurs, but discouraged from it** — "if you can work for someone else, do it." Todd's grandfather ran a tire store since 1946 (still bears the Frederickson name). Christen's mom was a serial entrepreneur/tailor.
3. **The catalyst (the founding story):** In ~2021-2022, a cascade — new baby (Charlotte, late 2021), Todd shattered his leg (airlifted, 5 months non-weight-bearing), then Christen was "unceremoniously laid off" (surprise "retirement") from her credit-union marketing job in Oct 2022 with a severance and a 10-month-old.
4. **Started on the couch** — Todd, immobile, found Cody Sanchez (buying/boring businesses) on YouTube. Brainstormed the company name at a doctor's office. "God put you on that couch because we just would not have done it."
5. **Launched Nov 2022**, first customer Dec 2022. **Under $5,000 total** to start. No bank loan — "we had the internet, our brains, and time." Family land next door = crucial support network.
6. **The model:** subcontractor/provider-based. "They don't work for us, we work with them." 50/50 split (vs. industry ~20%). Providers keep control of schedule and jobs. TVCT handles all "the boring stuff" — marketing, client management, scheduling, confrontation, payment.
7. **Core values:** quality over quantity; boundaries (Todd's most-repeated word); honesty/transparency; "we're going to make a mistake" + a guarantee; treat providers as humans/partners; find people with *heart*.
8. **Work-life balance:** 8–5, hard stop at 5 for "Dad mode"; learned boundaries after baby + accident; "money can't buy happiness but it can buy time."
9. **Marketing:** almost entirely organic — Facebook groups, BNI networking, Google LSA, Thumbtack, Yelp. Todd = "golden retriever energy" extrovert; Christen = behind-the-scenes, mom's groups/local groups. Veterans Day giveaway = most successful "objective." Realtor Association affiliate membership = clever moat (access to lockboxes; no other cleaners do it).
10. **Faith & gratitude** — devout Christians; Todd's father (a Vietnam vet) died right after Charlotte was born; Christen has been open about depression/mental-health struggles. "Cultivate gratitude… every day."
11. **Numbers:** ~$250K projected first-year revenue. 13 providers (17 counting teams), 1 office staff (VA, Michelle) + the two of them. Growing Google reviews 28→36 in ~2 weeks ("1% better every day").
12. **Vision:** 5 yrs = every major Alabama metro that meets criteria; 10 yrs = the Southeast.
13. **Tech:** BookingKoala (>50% of daily ops) + GoHighLevel ("Clean Works" version). Praises BookingKoala usability, provider app, reporting, media download, referral program.
14. **Advice to 10-yr-old self:** Christen — "you don't have to do all the things, and that's okay." Todd — "have fun while you can; grown-up life isn't figured out, you're just older and still confused."
