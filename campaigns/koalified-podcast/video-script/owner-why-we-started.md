# Owner Video Script — "Why We Started The Valley Clean Team"

> **This is a script for Todd to record himself on camera (optionally with Christen). It is NOT an AI-generated video — Todd is the real person, telling his real story in his own voice.**

**Speaker:** Todd (Christen optional for the tag line)
**Runtime target:** 60–90 seconds (full cut) + a 30-second cut-down below
**Delivery:** Warm, direct, a little humor. Talk to one person, not a crowd. Slow down on the hard parts.
**Source:** Real story beats from the Koalified Podcast — no embellishment.

---

## FULL CUT (60–90 seconds)

*Estimated spoken runtime at a natural, unhurried pace: ~80–85 seconds.*

[Shot: Todd, seated or standing, plain warm background. Eye contact with camera. Easy smile to open.]

Hey — I'm Todd. My wife Christen and I started The Valley Clean Team, and people ask us all the time how it began.

[Beat. Slight grin.]

Honestly? On a couch.

[Shot: relax the smile a little — this next part is sincere.]

For years I built businesses for other people. Then in 2022, everything hit at once. We had a brand-new baby girl. I shattered my leg — five months off my feet. And a few weeks after that, Christen got laid off.

[Beat.]

So there I am. Stuck on the couch. And I've got nothing but time to think.

[Shot: warmer, a little lighter.]

We started this company with under five thousand dollars. No loan. No office. Just the internet, our brains, and time. We're people of faith, and looking back — God put us on that couch. We just would not have done it otherwise.

[Shot: steady, a bit of conviction.]

Here's what makes us different. Our providers don't work *for* us — we work *with* them. We take care of the boring stuff so they can do great work, and so you get the same team, every time. It's better for them, and it's better for you. That's the whole point.

[Shot: land it. Calm, direct.]

Life is messy. We've got this. If you want your exact price, we're right here.

[Optional — Christen steps in or is beside him:]
[Christen]: Same team, every time.

[End card: The Valley Clean Team logo + "Get Your Price" + phone numbers.]
