We were guests on the Koalified Podcast recently, and telling our story out loud reminded us how much of it we'd never shared with the people who matter most — you, the families and businesses who let us into your homes and offices every week.

So here's the short version.

We're people of faith, and looking back, it's hard not to see it. As Todd likes to say, "God put you on that couch, because we just would not have done it" otherwise.

What we built is a little different from most cleaning companies. Our providers don't work *for* us — we work *with* them. They keep control of their schedules, and we handle the rest. When you get the same steady, cared-for team at your door, that's why.

We recorded the full conversation — the whole story, the mistakes, the faith, the family — for the Koalified Podcast.
