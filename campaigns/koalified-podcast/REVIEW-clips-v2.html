The master key to every house on the market

“You can't clean in the dark”

01:25:17~41s · 20s tight

Todd: “A lot of cleaners are like, oh, I would love to take that four p.m. start job for a three thousand square foot house. And I'm like, no, you can't clean in the dark. You can't clean windows. There's no sunlight, because that regular light don't work…”

Christen: “You can't clean in the dark.”

A genuinely surprising, physically true detail almost no homeowner knows — sunlight shows streaks lamp light hides. It also quietly explains a policy people push back on, without sounding like a rule. The most algorithm-friendly cut in the batch.

Walking away from the apartment complex
