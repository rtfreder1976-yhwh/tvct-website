## Post copy

Small-business tactic that actually worked for us: we joined the local realtors' association as an affiliate member. A couple hundred dollars a year.

It came with lockbox access to every MLS listing. So a realtor with a listing that needs cleaning before photos doesn't have to meet us there at all.

Nobody else in our category had joined yet.
