## Post copy

Here's something almost nobody knows about cleaning: you can't do it well in the dark.

Not "it's harder." You physically can't see what you're missing. Sunlight and lamp light hit a surface differently — streaks on a window, film on a countertop, dust along a baseboard all disappear under a regular bulb and reappear the next morning when the sun comes up.

Which is why we don't book a 3,000 square foot house for a 4 p.m. start in November. Not because we don't want the work. Because you'd be paying for a clean nobody could verify, including us.

Most people find that out the hard way, usually after they've already paid someone.
