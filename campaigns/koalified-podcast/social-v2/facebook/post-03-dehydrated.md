# Facebook — "Could you be dehydrated?"

The concrete version of everything the provider-first posts state abstractly. Pass 1 said "we put providers first" several times; this shows one person actually watching another.

---

## Post copy

Christen has a line about what happens when you stack too many jobs on one cleaner: "Oh, I forgot to have lunch. I'm in somebody's shower. I'm sorry — what did you say?"

That's what over-scheduling actually looks like from the inside. Not a policy violation. A person who skipped a meal and is now three hours into someone else's bathroom.

So we don't stack them. And when Todd notices someone slowing down partway through a day, the question he asks is "could you be dehydrated?"

It's not a management technique. It's just paying attention to a person who is doing hard physical work in a stranger's house.

That's also why we're careful about which jobs we take and how many we book in a day. It's not that we can't fill the calendar. It's that a cleaner on their fifth house does not do the same work as a cleaner on their second.
