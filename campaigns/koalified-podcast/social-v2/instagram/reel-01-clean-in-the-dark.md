## Caption

You can't clean in the dark.

Not "it's harder" — you physically can't see what you're missing. Streaks on glass, film on a counter, dust on a baseboard: all invisible under a lamp, all obvious the next morning.

So when someone asks for a 4 p.m. start on a big house in November, we say no. You'd be paying for a clean nobody could check.

Sunlight is a tool. It's just free.
