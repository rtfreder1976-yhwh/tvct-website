# LinkedIn — The realtors' association angle

**Why this one:** best teachable moment in the interview. Specific, replicable, and it has a genuine "wait, you can do that?" turn. Strong for the operator audience LinkedIn actually rewards.

---

## Post copy

We joined our local realtors' association as an affiliate member. When we joined, we were the only cleaning company in it.

The membership cost a couple hundred dollars. What it bought was lockbox access to every listed property on the MLS.

Think about what that removes. A realtor with a listing that needs a turn no longer has to drive across town to let a cleaner in, or coordinate a key handoff, or be physically present for a two-hour job. We show up, we do the work, we leave. The agent does nothing.

So when we're sitting in a room full of realtors and someone mentions a listing that needs attention before photos, the answer is yes — and we already have access.

The reaction is always the same. "Wait, you have a key to everything?"

The broader point isn't about cleaning. It's that we spent a couple hundred dollars on a small bet with a capped downside, and it changed how we get work. Most of what we've tried hasn't worked. This one did, and it was cheap enough that trying it was never really a risk.

Look for the room your competitors aren't in yet. The membership fee is usually the smallest part of what it costs to be there.
