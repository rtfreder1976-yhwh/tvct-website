# LinkedIn — The contract we walked away from

**Register:** measured, facility-manager-facing. No jokes, no residential CTA (voice-profile platform table).

---

## Post copy

A brand new apartment complex sent us a contract last year. Big building, real money, the kind of account that changes a quarter.

We read it. It made us responsible for air filters and the screens on every back porch — neither of which is cleaning — and it wasn't open to discussion.

Christen reads every contract before I do. She got about two pages in and said no.

Her reasoning is the part worth passing along: every contract is negotiable, and if a contract isn't negotiable, the people behind it aren't going to be good partners. A vendor agreement that can't survive one question in week one doesn't get more flexible in month six.

Nine times out of ten, that kind of account is a problem inside 60 days anyway. You just end up finding out after you've staffed it.

We'd rather scope a job honestly and both know what we agreed to.
