# IG 01 — Teach: the pillowcase trick

**Visual (creative kit):** photo-led — pillowcase sliding over a fan blade, mid-task, natural window light, peach cloth or peach brushstroke overlay as the single warm element. 1080x1350. Alternative: 3-slide carousel — slide 1 brushstroke card "Your fan has a winter setting.", slide 2 the switch close-up, slide 3 the pillowcase move.

**Caption:**

Your ceiling fan has a summer direction and a winter direction — the switch is right on the housing. Counterclockwise in summer pushes air down; clockwise in winter pulls it up.

While you're on the ladder: slide an old pillowcase over each blade and pull back slowly. The dust ends up in the case, not on the bed.
