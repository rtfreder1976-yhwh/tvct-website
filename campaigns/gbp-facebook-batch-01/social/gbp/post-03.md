# GBP 03 — Teach: granite vs. marble

Granite and marble are both stone, but they don't want the same cleaner. Granite is sealed — a neutral spray keeps the seal intact. Marble is softer and hates acid, so the vinegar trick that works on your faucet will etch it permanently. When in doubt, warm water and a microfiber cloth won't hurt either one. Knowing which bottle goes with which surface is a lot of what you're paying us for.
