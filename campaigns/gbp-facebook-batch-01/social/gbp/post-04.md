# GBP 04 — Teach: ceiling fan direction + the pillowcase trick

Your ceiling fan has a switch on the housing that reverses the blades. Counterclockwise in summer pushes air down; clockwise in winter pulls it up. While you're up there, slide an old pillowcase over each blade and pull back slowly — the dust ends up in the case instead of on the bed. That one's a freebie.
