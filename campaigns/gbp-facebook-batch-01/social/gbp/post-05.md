# GBP 05 — Teach: shower glass film

The cloudy film on shower glass usually isn't soap scum — it's minerals, left behind every time hard water dries. Soap scum wipes off. Mineral film has to be dissolved, which is why the all-purpose spray has never once won that fight. A squeegee after each shower keeps the film from forming at all. Thirty seconds a day, and the glass stays clear between our visits.
