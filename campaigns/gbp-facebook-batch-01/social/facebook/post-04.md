# FB 04 — Owners / why we built it this way

We started The Valley Clean Team after hearing the same story over and over: the cleaning company was fine at first, then the quality slid, then they stopped answering.

So we built the boring parts first. A 44-item checklist that runs the same way every visit. Background checks and $2 million in liability coverage behind every cleaner. A re-clean promise in writing: if we missed something, tell us within 24 hours and we'll come fix it, free.

Todd, a veteran, runs the company day to day. Christen makes sure you've heard of us. 1,500+ customers served across Alabama and Tennessee so far.
