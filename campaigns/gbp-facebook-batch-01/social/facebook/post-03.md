# FB 03 — Price transparency (how a quote usually goes)

Here's how a cleaning quote usually goes: a "starting at" number on the phone, then a different number once someone's seen your house.

Here's how ours goes: you tell us the square footage and the kind of clean you need, and we give you your exact price on that call. Not an hourly rate, not a guess — the number that shows up on your bill. It moves only if you add work or the job changes, and you'll hear from us before anything changes, not after.

We're not the cheapest. We are the ones who tell you the real number first.
