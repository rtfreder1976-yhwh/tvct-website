# FB 02 — Teach: shower glass film

That cloudy film on shower glass usually isn't soap scum. It's minerals — hard water dries, the water leaves, the minerals stay. Soap scum wipes off with almost anything. Mineral film has to be dissolved, which is why the all-purpose spray has never once won that fight.

Two things work: a squeegee after every shower, which stops the film from forming, or a cleaner made to dissolve minerals once it's there.

The squeegee habit costs thirty seconds. The rest of the bathroom is on our 44-item checklist — the shower is only one line of it.
