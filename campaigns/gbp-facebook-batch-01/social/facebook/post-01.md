# FB 01 — Teach: ceiling fan direction + the pillowcase trick

Your ceiling fan has a summer direction and a winter direction. The switch is on the housing: counterclockwise in summer pushes air down, clockwise in winter pulls it up off the ceiling. Flip it twice a year and your thermostat works less.

While you're on the ladder, slide an old pillowcase over each blade and pull back slowly — the dust ends up inside the case instead of on the bed.
